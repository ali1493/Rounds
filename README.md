# Rounds

A volunteer driver's companion app for Meals on Wheels delivery operations.

**Track:** Food Security Delivery Operations
**Event:** BuildersVault Social Services Hackathon, Victoria BC, April 2026

## What this is

Rounds gives volunteer drivers real agency over their shifts while the system
enforces the safety rules — severe allergies, wheelchair accessibility,
closure/deceased status — that today live only in the head of whichever
long-time volunteer happens to be on shift.

Designed for a first-shift volunteer who has no tribal knowledge, using a
mobile-web interface that makes safety-critical information legible at the
point of care.
